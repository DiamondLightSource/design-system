## Communicate state clearly

Provide feedback for important actions, transitions and system events. Users
should understand:

- what is happening
- whether an action succeeded
- whether attention is required
- whether work is still in progress
- what they can safely do next

Avoid relying on colour alone. Combine colour with labels, icons, text or layout
changes where appropriate.

## Progress and transitional states

Use informational feedback for active or changing system states. Examples
include:

- scanning in progress
- moving motors
- connecting to a device
- processing data
- queue paused

Prefer persistent inline or contextual feedback over temporary notifications for
long-running operations.

## Success feedback

Use success feedback sparingly. Not every successful action needs a
notification. Prefer lightweight confirmation for routine actions, especially in
repetitive scientific workflows.
